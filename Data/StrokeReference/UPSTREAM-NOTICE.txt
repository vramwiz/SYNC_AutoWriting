Statement of changes
--------------------
1. Restricted the dataset to Japanese kana; all kanji were removed.
2. Converted stroke geometry from SVG path descriptions to JSON.
3. Resampled each stroke into an ordered sequence of points.
4. Computed, per stroke, a dominant direction vector and a closed-loop flag.
5. Normalised all coordinates into a unit-square reference frame.

Stroke count and stroke order are unchanged from the original data.
No stroke was added, removed, or reordered.
